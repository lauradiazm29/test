    Methodology, coverage and sources

    What this application shows

    Four indicator modules computed from open data published by the Spanish Ministry of the Interior and by the National Statistics Institute (INE).

    Coverage: the most important limitation

    The Ministry of the Interior publishes municipal crime data only for municipalities with a population of more than 20,000 inhabitants. The Community of Madrid has 179 municipalities and only a minority of them appear in the source.

    Notes on individual indicators

    • Module A. The rate uses the resident population of the municipality as the denominator, so it does not correct for commuting, tourism or any other daytime population. Municipalities that attract many non residents therefore appear worse than a per capita reading would justify.
    • Module B. Recorded and cleared offences refer to the same reporting period but not necessarily to the same offences, since an offence recorded in one year can be cleared in the next.
    • Module C. Victims are recorded from age 0, arrested or investigated persons from age 14. The two distributions are therefore only comparable over the five age bands from 14 to 17 upwards. Victim records also include an unknown sex category with no population denominator, and the rate per 10,000 inhabitants is defined only for the total over age bands, because the population is available broken down by sex and not by age band.
    • Module D. Between 37 and 79 per cent of recorded offences fall in the residual category "other offences", which mixes violent and non violent conduct and cannot be assigned to either class.

    Sources and attribution

    • Crime data: Ministerio del Interior, Portal Estadístico de Criminalidad.
    • Population: Instituto Nacional de Estadística. Elaboración propia con datos extraídos del sitio web del INE.
    • Municipal surface areas, used as the denominator of the offences per km² indicator: Nomenclátor Geográfico de Municipios y Entidades de Población, CC BY 4.0 ign.es. Areas are published in hectares and converted to square kilometres.
    • Municipal boundaries: obra derivada de Líneas Límite Municipales, IDEM Comunidad de Madrid, CC BY 4.0. Reprojected from ETRS89 UTM zone 30N to WGS 84 and dissolved to one polygon per municipality.
